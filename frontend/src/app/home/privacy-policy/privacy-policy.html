1. Introduction

At Techzaz EDR, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users' data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose personal and telemetry data when you use the Techzaz EDR Platform.

By accessing or using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Our platform is designed to provide next-generation endpoint detection and response, which requires the collection of specific system telemetry to detect and prevent threats.

2. Data Collection

2.1 Telemetry Data

To provide effective security services, our agents collect endpoint telemetry including:

  • Process execution and behavior patterns
  • Network connection metadata (IP addresses, ports)
  • File system activity and modifications
  • System configuration and hardware identifiers
  • Memory artifacts for threat analysis

2.2 Personal Information

We collect personal information necessary for account management and security:

  • Name and email address for authentication
  • Organization details and user roles
  • IP addresses of management console users

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

Threat Detection

Analyzing behavioral patterns to identify and block malicious activity in real-time.

Incident Response

Providing visual timelines and attack chains for security investigation.

Platform Analytics

Aggregating risk scores to provide an overview of your security posture.

Service Improvement

Refining our detection engines and improving agent performance.

4. Data Security

Data security is at the core of Techzaz EDR. We implement industry-leading measures to protect your data:

End-to-end encryption for all agent-to-server communication.
Multi-tenant isolation ensuring data is never leaked between organizations.
Regular security audits and automated vulnerability scanning.
Role-based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring minimum privilege.

5. Your Rights

You have certain rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You can request detailed information about what data we hold.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request the removal of specific telemetry data after established retention periods.
  • Right to Portability: You can export security logs for use in your own SIEM or SOAR tools.

To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy team at privacy@techzaz.com.
